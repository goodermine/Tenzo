# Credits

TILT LAB has no third-party art or audio. Every shape is drawn in code
(`src/render/`), the icons by `tools/make-icons.mjs`, and every sound and the
music are synthesised (`src/audio/audioManager.ts`).

## Font

[Fredoka](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Fredoka), SIL Open Font License
(`fonts/OFL-Fredoka.txt`), bundled in `fonts/`.

## Libraries

- [planck.js](https://github.com/piqnt/planck.js) (MIT) — 2D physics, a
  JavaScript port of Box2D; bundled into `dist/tilt-lab.js`.
- [esbuild](https://esbuild.github.io) (MIT), [TypeScript](https://www.typescriptlang.org)
  (Apache 2.0) and [sharp](https://sharp.pixelplumbing.com) (Apache 2.0) —
  build-time only.
